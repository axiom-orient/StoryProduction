# STEP 6 — Scene Transition Blueprint

Turn the approved treatment into canonical `SCN-*` transition contracts. Every material scene entry must contain:

`SCN-###. INT./EXT. PLACE - TIME`
- Source lineage: SRC/REL/GAP/transformation IDs
- Necessary function: source/state/information obligation this scene must perform
- Cinematic value: the specific behavior, image, sound, spatial pressure, relationship event or tension that makes this realization worth watching
- Required preconditions: location, physical state, knowledge/belief, relationship/power, props/access, permissions, open promises
- Immediate task / pressure
- Observable action / choice
- Dominant turn
- Required postconditions / state writes
- Information delta: truth / character / audience, including acquisition path for material new knowledge
- World-rule use: relevant trigger/scope/effect/limit if any
- Spatial or physical constraint
- Downstream dependencies: later SCN IDs or obligations enabled
- Risk: `LOW|WATCH|HIGH` + categories `FIDELITY|CAUSAL|KNOWLEDGE|RULE|STATE|PHYSICAL|INVENTION|RESEARCH|FORM|ENDING`

A scene may contain several events. It must produce a material turn or serve a justified cinematic function. A scene that is merely necessary exposition but has no cinematic value must be combined, redesigned or externalized rather than preserved as prose-shaped explanation.
