# STEP 5 — Causal Treatment

Write the whole screenplay as a causal treatment using approved Source/Narrative/Execution/Character contracts.

The treatment must make visible:
- why each major event happens now;
- actor ownership of material decisions;
- state and knowledge changes that enable later events;
- world-rule use without changing trigger/scope/effect/limits;
- externalization of material interior or interpretive source meaning;
- the approved expansion budget: `DRAMATIZE/CONNECT/EXPAND/INVENT` decisions remain traceable;
- ending event, agency, semantic reframe and residue.

When adaptation pressure is high, prefer a smaller number of justified expansions over filler subplots. Do not turn source compression into permission to invent.
