# STEP 3 — Cinematic Execution System

Design only cinematic constraints needed to execute the approved Narrative Map.

For material spaces and recurring forms record:
- topology, entrances/exits, thresholds, access, visibility, audibility, ownership/control;
- physical or institutional constraints that can change action;
- recurring image/sound/form only when it performs a narrative function;
- source-form translation for any `SUPPORTING|ESSENTIAL` form;
- externalization carriers for material interior/narrated meaning;
- research questions whose factual answer could change plot, behavior, procedure, space or safety.

Do not add motif quotas, decorative visual systems or director imitation. A cinematic choice must earn its place by carrying information, pressure, behavior, state or meaning.
