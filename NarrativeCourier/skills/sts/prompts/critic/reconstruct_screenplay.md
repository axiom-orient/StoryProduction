# STEP 8C — Source-Blind Reverse Reconstruction

From the screenplay alone reconstruct the story actually present on the page.

Required:
- `OBS-*` material assertions with actor, polarity, epistemic status, evidence SCN and confidence;
- `OREL-*` causal/temporal/knowledge/identity/relationship/world-rule relations with evidence;
- recovered world-rule behavior and material knowledge-acquisition paths;
- recovered ending event, decisive actor(s), semantic reframe and residue/open question;
- recovered screen-form functions when UI/text/VO/document/chat/montage or another form materially carries meaning;
- recovered knowledge/reveal ledger;
- material scene transition/state contradictions found internally;
- uncertainty where the screenplay does not support a confident inference.

Write each recovered assertion or relation as its own Markdown bullet beginning with its ID. Keep its
one-or-more screenplay scene citations in that same item using `evidence=SCN-001` (comma-separate
multiple scene IDs). Every recovered item needs evidence from the screenplay itself; do not create
an item from source memory.

Never infer unavailable `SRC-*`/`REL-*` IDs, source wording or intended adaptation rationale.
