# Checkpoint A — Narrative Contract Review

Try to falsify the Screenplay Contract and Narrative IR against the approved Source IR.

Prioritize:
- protected assertion/relation drift;
- unsupported `INVENT` or expansion hidden as connective detail;
- whether `Adaptation Pressure` is honestly represented for the requested target duration;
- WORLD_RULE scope/trigger/effect drift;
- ending outcome, agency and semantic-reframe drift;
- actor/epistemic changes and missing knowledge acquisition;
- loss of SUPPORTING/ESSENTIAL source-form function;
- causal gaps and accidental audience-information confusion.

Classify findings `CRITICAL|MAJOR|MINOR` with evidence and specific revision target. Do not score formula compliance.
