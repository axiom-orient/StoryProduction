# STEP 2 — Narrative Map

Convert the approved source and screenplay contract into a presentation map without changing protected story truth.

Required sections:

- `## Fabula` — material events in story-world causal/temporal order.
- `## Syuzhet` — proposed audience presentation order and why it serves the audience contract.
- `## Information Ledger` — for each material reveal: truth state, character knowledge/belief before and after, audience knowledge before and after, and acquisition path (`SOURCE_GIVEN|OBSERVED|TOLD|INFERRED|REMEMBERED|UNKNOWN`).
- `## Transformation Ledger` — every material `PRESERVE|DRAMATIZE|CONNECT|REORDER|COMBINE|OMIT|EXPAND|INVENT` decision with Source IDs and authority. `INVENT` must cite Human-approved GAP/authorization.
- `## Externalization Ledger` — material internal/narrated/abstract/coda meaning and its proposed observable or otherwise justified screen carrier.
- `## Open Risks` — unresolved causal, epistemic, rule, form, expansion or ending risks.

Do not treat nonlinear presentation as causal change. Do not allow presentation order to leak future knowledge into characters.
