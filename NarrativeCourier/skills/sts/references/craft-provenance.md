# Craft Provenance — Research, Not Execution Modes

STS does not imitate filmmakers. The sources below were used only to extract reusable, higher-order craft constraints.

- **Bong Joon-ho — BAFTA Screenwriters’ Lecture (2019):** character through concrete behavior and interaction; space as an active dramatic system; genre conventions can be honored or broken when reality demands it. https://www.bafta.org/media-centre/press-releases/screenwriters-lecture-series-2019-bong-joon-ho/
- **Park Chan-wook — BAFTA Screenwriters’ Lecture (2016):** cinematic form exists to serve the story rather than decoration. https://www.bafta.org/media-centre/press-releases/bafta-screenwriters-lecture-series-park-chan-wook/
- **Charlie Kaufman — BAFTA Screenwriters’ Lecture (2011):** skepticism toward universal screenplay formulas supports treating structure templates as heuristics, not truth. https://www.bafta.org/media-centre/press-releases/screenwriters-lecture-charlie-kaufman/
- **Peter Straughan — BAFTA Screenwriters’ Lecture (2012):** adaptation is re-imagining source in a different medium, supporting semantic rather than lexical fidelity. https://www.bafta.org/media-centre/press-releases/screenwriters-lecture-peter-straughan/
- **Ronald Harwood — BAFTA Screenwriters’ Lecture (2010):** adaptation must preserve the essential source while becoming cinema. https://www.bafta.org/media-centre/press-releases/screenwriters-lecture-sir-ronald-harwood-cbe/
- **Christopher Nolan / Dunkirk — DGA Quarterly (2017):** form and temporal structure were chosen to produce a particular audience experience, supporting “structure follows experience.” https://www.dga.org/craft/dgaq/issues/1703-summer-2017/wwii-dunkirk
- **Alfred Hitchcock — Sight and Sound, “my own methods”:** audience information and clarity are active suspense tools, supporting explicit knowledge/reveal state. https://www.bfi.org.uk/sight-and-sound/features/alfred-hitchcock-my-own-methods
- **Sidney Lumet — DGA Visual History:** preparation and dramatic intention support treating screenplay execution as deliberate, testable choices rather than surface style. https://www.dga.org/Craft/VisualHistory/Interviews/Sidney-Lumet

These references are provenance only and are intentionally not routed into generation prompts.
