# Space / Cinematic Form

- Space is a dramatic machine: topology, access, visibility, audibility, thresholds, hidden areas and ownership can enable or block action.
- Image, sound, framing, transition and motif require a narrative function; no quota exists.
- Use camera-direction language only when POV, reveal or spatial relation is itself story information.
- A screenplay addition should be physically stageable. If an action depends on a location/object/access rule, establish that rule before use.
