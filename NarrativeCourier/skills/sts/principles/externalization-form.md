# Cinematic Externalization & Source Form

Adaptation preserves **semantic function**, not necessarily prose wording or document container.

## Externalization

Classify source meaning that is not already observable:

- internal thought;
- narrated interpretation;
- compressed time/history;
- off-screen knowledge;
- abstract world rule;
- semantic coda / final reframe.

For each material item choose the smallest cinematic carrier that preserves meaning:

`behavior | consequence | spatial relation | image | sound | dialogue | silence | text/UI | voice-over | montage | other justified form`.

Voice-over and text are allowed when they are the best carrier; they are not automatic failures. Exposition is a problem only when it duplicates or weakens the dramatic evidence.

## Source-form function

Classify the source container:

- `NEUTRAL` — wording/form can change freely if semantics survive;
- `SUPPORTING` — form contributes tone, information control or rhythm;
- `ESSENTIAL` — the form itself performs a material narrative function.

An `ESSENTIAL` form does not require literal reproduction. It requires a screen-native equivalent that performs the same function.

## Semantic coda

A prose ending may add a second layer of meaning after the event. Do not delete it merely because film should “show, not tell.” Record:

- event outcome;
- agency owner;
- semantic reframe;
- residue / unresolved question.

The screenplay may externalize the reframe without reproducing the sentence. Final verification asks whether a cold reader can recover the same material meaning.

## Scene desirability

A scene that is logically necessary is not automatically worth shooting. For every material scene identify:

- `Necessary function` — what obligation/state transition it performs;
- `Cinematic value` — the behavior, image, sound, tension, spatial action or relationship event that makes this specific realization worth watching.

If a needed scene has no cinematic value, combine, redesign or externalize it rather than preserving prose-shaped exposition.
