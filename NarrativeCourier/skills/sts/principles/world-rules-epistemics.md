# World Rules & Epistemic Ownership

Speculative rules and character knowledge are executable state, not decoration.

## WORLD_RULE facets

For every material `REL-*` of type `WORLD_RULE`, recover enough of the following to prevent silent rule drift:

- trigger;
- scope / affected entities;
- effect;
- cost or tradeoff, if stated;
- exceptions / limits;
- reversibility / duration;
- who knows the rule and with what authority;
- source evidence.

Unknown facets remain `UNKNOWN`; do not fill them merely to make the world feel complete.

## Knowledge acquisition

A material belief or fact cannot appear in a character's possession without a traceable path. Track:

`prior state → acquisition event/source → resulting KNOWS/BELIEVES state`.

Valid acquisition modes include `SOURCE_GIVEN | OBSERVED | TOLD | INFERRED | REMEMBERED | UNKNOWN`.

Do not convert:

- BELIEF into FACT;
- CLAIM into FACT;
- audience knowledge into character knowledge;
- one character's knowledge into another's;
- later knowledge into earlier knowledge.

## Rule/state verification

Before a scene uses a rule, prop, permission, injury, location, secret or relationship state, verify that the required state exists. If the source leaves it open, route it through GAP/adaptation authority rather than smuggling it in as fact.
