# Source Fidelity

1. Preserve material assertions as typed obligations: actor, action/state, target, polarity, epistemic status and outcome.
2. Preserve material relations, not only event coverage. `A` and `B` surviving does not preserve `A CAUSES B`.
3. Treat ending outcome, decisive actor ownership, identity, irreversible knowledge and explicit negation as high-sensitivity fields.
4. `FACT`, `BELIEF`, `CLAIM` and `UNKNOWN` are not interchangeable.
5. Record legitimate absence as `GAP-*` and conflicting authority as `CON-*`; do not silently resolve either.
6. New screenplay detail is allowed when it occupies open adaptation space and does not contradict protected meaning.
7. Transformations are classified as `PRESERVE`, `DRAMATIZE`, `REORDER`, `COMBINE`, `OMIT`, or `INVENT`, with rationale and source lineage.
8. An explicit source unknown is itself a source assertion, not an adaptation gap. Preserve which cause, agent, identity, time, or explanation remains unknown whenever that uncertainty changes blame, mystery, or ending meaning.
