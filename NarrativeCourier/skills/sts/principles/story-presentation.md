# Story / Presentation

- Keep **fabula** (what happens) separate from **syuzhet** (how the audience receives it).
- Structure follows the source material and intended audience experience; formula is diagnostic only.
- For each material reveal track: truth, character knowledge/belief, audience knowledge/belief and reveal timing.
- Nonlinear presentation is valid when chronology, causality and material relations remain recoverable.
- Withholding information must create an intended experience, not accidental confusion.
