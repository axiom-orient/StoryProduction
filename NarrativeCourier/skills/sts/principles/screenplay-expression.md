# Screenplay Expression

- Write visible/audible present-tense action and playable behavior.
- Canonical internal scene identity is `SCN-###. INT./EXT. PLACE - TIME`.
- Use scene headings, action, character, parenthetical only when useful, and dialogue as readable screenplay elements; typography itself is not a fidelity score.
- Give necessary exposition through the clearest dramatic channel once; avoid repeating the same fact through multiple redundant channels.
- Do not write unfilmable internal explanation unless converted into behavior, sound, image or an explicit narrative device authorized by the screenplay contract.
