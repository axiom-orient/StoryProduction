# Research / Reality

Research only where factual error can materially change plot, behavior, procedure, profession, location, technology, law, culture, physical feasibility or social reality.

- Prefer one exact, plot-relevant detail to many generic details.
- Mark unresolved factual uncertainty instead of hallucinating precision.
- References may constrain source interpretation only when the Human establishes their authority.
- Research supports the story; it does not replace source obligations.
