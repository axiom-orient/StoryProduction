# Optional Heuristics — Never Correctness Gates

Use only when they diagnose a concrete problem: three-act structure, sequences, 15 beats, midpoint placement, page/scene counts, motif counts, conflict density, dialogue ratios, protagonist arc models.

A heuristic violation is not a failure. A work may use custom, episodic, circular, parallel or nonlinear structure if its causal/information contract is coherent and source fidelity is preserved.
