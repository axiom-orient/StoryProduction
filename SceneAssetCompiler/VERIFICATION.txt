The standalone verifier checks ZIP CRC, regular-file sizes and hashes,
executable bits, deterministic rebuild byte identity, extracted full unittest
discovery (105 total: 104 executed and 1 opt-in skip), PerfectPixel schema and
transparent/opaque inspect fixtures, a v2 provider negative probe with process
custody, and an actual extracted SAC integration smoke using the built
PerfectPixel and a v2 fake provider. The archive digest and byte count are
authoritative only in the adjacent release record and checksum sidecar; they
are intentionally not repeated here to avoid a source-manifest cycle.

The provider contract is strict `misegen.provider/2`: preflight hashes the
exact binary, observes its bounded v2 invalid_request probe, and rejects v1,
malformed, non-v2, timeout, or stale-hash cases before dispatch. A successful
PNG imports only hash/byte-count/dimension/alpha-bound PerfectPixel inspect
evidence. `UNKNOWN` and timeout attempts are terminal and have immediate and
delayed no-survivor checks.

The canonical public commands are `execute-scene-provider` and
`probe-scene-provider`. Historical command spellings are argv-level hidden
compatibility aliases and are absent from normal help. Local diagnostics and
receipt notes use `SCENE_PROVIDER_*`/`scene_provider.*`; upstream
`VERGERAIL_*` environment variables, the upstream runtime verification flag,
and historical 0.12 evidence names remain only at their required compatibility
boundaries.
